# AI Lead Qualification & Follow-Up

> Instantly qualify, score, and nurture new leads so sales teams only speak to the right prospects.

---

## Problem

Slow or inconsistent follow-up is one of the biggest reasons good leads go cold.  
Manual qualification also creates delays and uneven lead quality for sales teams.

---

## Solution

An automated lead handling system that:

- Captures new leads the moment they arrive (form, webhook, or inbox)
- Uses AI + business rules to qualify and score them
- Routes hot leads to the right person
- Triggers personalised follow-up sequences
- Offers booking links at the right moment
- Keeps the CRM updated in real time

---

## How It Works

1. **Trigger** – New lead enters the system  
2. **AI Processing** – Intent, fit, and urgency are assessed  
3. **Scoring & Routing** – Lead is scored and assigned  
4. **Automated Follow-Up** – Personalised sequence begins  
5. **CRM Update** – Status and activity are logged  
6. **Human Handoff** – Sales takes over when the lead is ready  

---

## Key Components

- Lead capture (forms, webhooks, email parsing)
- Qualification criteria and scoring logic
- AI-generated personalised messaging
- Multi-step follow-up sequences
- Calendar / booking integration
- CRM lifecycle updates

---

## Typical Tech Stack

- **Orchestration:** n8n  
- **AI:** Claude / OpenAI  
- **CRM:** HubSpot / GoHighLevel / Salesforce  
- **Booking:** Calendly / Cal.com / Google Calendar  

---

## Business Impact

- Faster speed-to-lead
- Higher conversion rates
- Better use of sales time
- Cleaner and more reliable pipeline data
- Reduced manual follow-up effort

---

## Who This Is For

- Sales and marketing teams with inbound lead flow
- Agencies and service businesses
- Companies using HubSpot, GoHighLevel, or Salesforce
- Teams that want consistent, fast follow-up without hiring more SDRs
