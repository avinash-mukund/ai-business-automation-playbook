# CRM Automation

> Keep your CRM clean, consistent, and useful by automating the processes that usually rely on manual effort.

---

## Problem

Manual data entry, inconsistent lifecycle stages, and poor data quality quietly undermine the value of most CRMs. Sales and marketing teams end up working with incomplete or unreliable information.

---

## Solution

A structured CRM automation layer that handles:

- Contact and deal creation
- Lifecycle stage progression
- Task and notification triggers
- Data quality enforcement
- Synchronisation between the CRM and other systems

---

## How It Works

1. **Trigger** – New contact, form submission, or system event  
2. **Create / Update** – Records are created or enriched  
3. **Lifecycle Logic** – Stage progression rules are applied  
4. **Quality Checks** – Key fields and data standards are enforced  
5. **Actions** – Tasks, notifications, or sync jobs are triggered  
6. **Visibility** – Teams work from cleaner, more reliable data  

---

## Key Components

- Object and lifecycle modelling
- Workflow automation
- Data quality rules
- Cross-system field sync
- Task and notification logic
- Audit-friendly logging

---

## Typical Tech Stack

- **CRM:** HubSpot / Salesforce / GoHighLevel  
- **Orchestration:** n8n  
- **Supporting Tools:** Webhooks, APIs, Google Workspace  

---

## Business Impact

- Cleaner and more trustworthy CRM data
- Less manual admin for sales and marketing
- More reliable reporting and forecasting
- Better alignment between teams
- Faster onboarding of new processes

---

## Who This Is For

- Sales and marketing teams using HubSpot, Salesforce, or GoHighLevel
- Operations teams tired of cleaning CRM data
- Companies scaling lead and customer processes
- Teams that need consistent lifecycle management
