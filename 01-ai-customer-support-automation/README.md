# AI Customer Support Automation

> Reduce support workload and response times by letting AI handle repetitive enquiries while escalating the complex ones.

---

## Problem

Support teams spend a large portion of their time on repetitive, predictable questions. This slows response times, increases cost per ticket, and leaves agents with less capacity for high-value work.

---

## Solution

An AI-powered support system that:

- Classifies incoming messages by intent and urgency
- Retrieves relevant knowledge or past customer context
- Drafts or sends accurate responses
- Escalates complex or sensitive issues to human agents
- Logs every interaction cleanly in the CRM or helpdesk

---

## How It Works

1. **Trigger** – New support message arrives (email, chat, form)
2. **Classify** – AI determines intent and urgency
3. **Retrieve** – Relevant knowledge or history is pulled
4. **Respond** – AI drafts or sends a reply
5. **Escalate** – Complex cases are routed to a human
6. **Log** – Outcome is recorded in the CRM / helpdesk

---

## Key Components

- Intent and urgency classification
- Knowledge base / retrieval
- Response generation
- Escalation rules
- CRM or helpdesk updates
- Confidence thresholds for human handoff

---

## Typical Tech Stack

- **Orchestration:** n8n  
- **AI:** Claude / OpenAI  
- **Support / CRM:** HubSpot, Zendesk, Intercom, Freshdesk  

---

## Business Impact

- Faster first response times
- Lower cost per ticket
- More consistent tone and quality
- Agents freed up for complex issues
- Better visibility of support patterns

---

## Who This Is For

- Customer support and success teams
- Businesses with high volumes of repetitive enquiries
- Companies using modern helpdesk or CRM tools
- Teams that want to scale support without linear headcount growth
