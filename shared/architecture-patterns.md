# Common Architecture Patterns

Most systems in this playbook follow a similar structure:

1. **Trigger** (webhook, form, email, call, schedule)
2. **AI Processing** (classify, extract, decide, generate)
3. **Business Logic** (rules, scoring, routing)
4. **System Actions** (CRM update, calendar, message, ticket)
5. **Human-in-the-loop** (escalation or approval when needed)
6. **Logging & Measurement**
