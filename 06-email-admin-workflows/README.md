# Email & Administrative Workflows

> Turn repetitive operational email into structured, automated processes that save time and reduce missed requests.

---

## Problem

Operational email creates a constant stream of repetitive work — classification, data entry, routing, and approvals. Important requests get buried, and teams waste hours on low-value admin.

---

## Solution

Intelligent email workflows that:

- Parse and classify incoming emails
- Extract key information automatically
- Route requests to the right person or process
- Update spreadsheets, CRM records, or documents
- Trigger approval or notification flows when needed

---

## How It Works

1. **Email Received** – Message arrives in a monitored inbox  
2. **Parse & Classify** – AI extracts intent and key fields  
3. **Route** – Request is sent to the correct workflow or owner  
4. **Update Systems** – Sheets, CRM, or documents are updated  
5. **Notify / Approve** – Relevant people are alerted or asked to approve  
6. **Log** – Action history is recorded for visibility  

---

## Key Components

- Email parsing and classification
- Information extraction (name, request type, dates, etc.)
- Routing logic
- System updates (CRM, Sheets, docs)
- Approval and notification flows
- Error handling and fallbacks

---

## Typical Tech Stack

- **Orchestration:** n8n  
- **AI:** Claude / OpenAI  
- **Email:** Gmail / Outlook  
- **Other:** Google Sheets, HubSpot, internal tools  

---

## Business Impact

- Hours saved every week on admin work
- Fewer missed or delayed requests
- Faster internal turnaround
- Better consistency in how requests are handled
- Clearer audit trail of actions taken

---

## Who This Is For

- Operations and admin teams
- Businesses handling high volumes of internal or customer email requests
- Companies that want to reduce manual data entry
- Teams looking to standardise repetitive processes
