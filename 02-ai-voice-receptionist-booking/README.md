# AI Voice Receptionist & Appointment Booking

## Problem
Missed calls and manual booking processes lead to lost enquiries and heavy admin load, especially for clinics, agencies, and professional services.

## Solution
An AI voice agent that:
- Answers calls 24/7
- Understands booking, rescheduling, or enquiry intent
- Checks calendar availability
- Books the appointment
- Updates the CRM
- Sends confirmation to both customer and business

## Typical Stack
Vapi / Retell + n8n + Google Calendar / Cal.com + HubSpot / GoHighLevel

## Business Impact
- Fewer missed opportunities
- Reduced front-desk workload
- Instant booking confirmation
- Cleaner CRM data
