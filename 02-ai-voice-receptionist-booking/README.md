# AI Voice Receptionist & Appointment Booking

> Never miss a call. Automatically answer, understand intent, book appointments, and update your CRM.

---

## Problem

Missed calls and manual appointment booking create lost opportunities and heavy admin load — especially for clinics, agencies, consultancies, and service businesses.

---

## Solution

An AI voice receptionist that:

- Answers incoming calls 24/7
- Understands the caller’s intent (book, reschedule, enquire)
- Collects the required information
- Checks calendar availability
- Books the appointment
- Updates the CRM
- Sends confirmation to both the customer and the business

---

## How It Works

1. **Incoming Call** – AI voice agent answers  
2. **Intent Detection** – Booking, reschedule, or general enquiry  
3. **Information Collection** – Name, preferred time, service, etc.  
4. **Calendar Check** – Availability is verified  
5. **Booking Confirmed** – Appointment is created  
6. **CRM + Notifications** – Record updated and confirmations sent  

---

## Key Components

- AI Voice Agent (Vapi / Retell-style)
- Speech-to-text and intent classification
- Calendar integration
- CRM contact & deal/appointment creation
- Confirmation messaging (SMS/Email)
- Fallback to human when needed

---

## Typical Tech Stack

- **Voice:** Vapi / Retell  
- **Orchestration:** n8n  
- **AI:** Claude / OpenAI  
- **Calendar:** Google Calendar / Cal.com  
- **CRM:** HubSpot / GoHighLevel / Salesforce  

---

## Business Impact

- Fewer missed leads
- Reduced front-desk / admin workload
- Instant appointment confirmation
- Cleaner CRM data
- Better customer experience outside business hours
